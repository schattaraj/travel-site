Kolkata — The City of Joy

North Calcutta is one of India's most layered urban experiences. Behind its crumbling facades are ancestral homes with courtyards still echoing with the music of centuries past.

We take you past the tourist corridors into a Calcutta that few outsiders reach: the ink-stained printing presses of College Street, the idol-makers of Kumortuli, the sweetshop owners whose family recipe has fed the neighbourhood for five generations.

This is a city of intellectual traditions, revolutionary history, and a warmth so profound it becomes physical — felt in handshakes, in shared plates, in the way a stranger in a lane will stop to ask where you're going and make sure you get there.
