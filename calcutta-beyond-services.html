Solo Adventures

Unscripted Travel,
Deeply Personal

Designed for the traveller who has outgrown package tours. Our solo journeys are co-created with you — built around your curiosities, your rhythm, and the Bengal you want to find.

You'll stay with families, wake up to paddy fields, cycle through canal-side villages, and sit with potters whose craft traces back centuries. Nothing is staged. Everything is real.

  • Personalised pre-trip consultation with a local expert
  • Handpicked community homestays with local families
  • Artisan workshops: pottery, weaving, cooking
  • Private guided heritage walks through old Calcutta
  • 24/7 support with dedicated trip concierge

Local Life
Local Life & Departure

Become Part of the
Community, Briefly

The most meaningful travel is when the boundary between tourist and local dissolves. You spend your mornings in the market with the fisherman, your afternoons with the weaver, and your evenings around a shared fire.

This isn't observation. It's participation. You leave having contributed something — and received something that no souvenir shop can offer.

  • Village immersion with pre-assigned local family hosts
  • Participate in daily rural rituals and harvesting seasons
  • Contribute to community development micro-projects
  • Learn traditional skills: terracotta art, jute weaving, mustard oil pressing
  • Personalised farewell with community ceremony

Our Itinerary

Kolkata Tour
Itinerary Highlights

A glimpse into the journeys we craft — each one a living story, built from Calcutta's layered history, Bengal's rural soul, and the warmth of its people.

Day 1

Arrival & City Exploration

Met at Dum Dum airport by your personal guide. Check-in to heritage guest house in South Calcutta. Evening walk through Park Street's colonial lanes and a welcome dinner of authentic Bengali cuisine.

Heritage Walk Bengali Cuisine Colonial Calcutta
Day 2

Heritage & Culture Deep Dive

Morning at Kumortuli watching masters sculpt the goddess. Afternoon at the Indian Museum and College Street — Asia's largest second-hand book market. Evening boat on the Hooghly at sunset.

Artisans Museum River at Sunset
Day 3

Local Life & Departure

Early morning at Mullick Ghat flower market — Calcutta at its most alive. Cooking class with a local family. Afternoon free for final exploration. Evening transfer to airport with a full heart and new friendships.
