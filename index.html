Welcome to Calcutta & Beyond

Calcutta and Beyond is an initiative for an active participation in rural lifestyle. Ideal for rest, rejuvenation, relaxation and discovery — enabling interactions between the tourists and the locals for a more enriching tourism experience.

Calcutta and Beyond is an experience where you are not just a tourist but you become a part of the community, you interact, discover, exchange and leave rejuvenated and enriched.
