Who We Are

Born from a Love
for Authentic Bengal

Calcutta & Beyond was founded in 2012 by a group of local storytellers, guides, and community advocates who believed that travel should leave places better — not just give visitors a great time.

We are a Kolkata-based travel company specialising in immersive, sustainable journeys through West Bengal and beyond. Every itinerary we design carries the fingerprints of the communities that made them possible.

Our team of local experts, community liaisons, and heritage scholars collaborate with you to create journeys that go far beneath the surface — into kitchens, craft studios, monsoon fields, and morning markets that tourists rarely reach.

The Principles Behind
Every Journey We Design

These aren't words on a wall — they're the criteria we apply to every decision we make for every traveller who trusts us.

Authentic Places

We curate destinations untouched by mass tourism — real places where real stories unfold every single day.

Community First

Every rupee you spend flows directly back into the communities that host you. Travel that uplifts, not extracts.

Why Calcutta & Beyond

We Know This Land
Like No One Else

Our team has spent over a decade building relationships with village communities, heritage property owners, artisan cooperatives, and rural families across Bengal. That depth of connection is something no outsider can replicate.

  • Deep access to private estates, heritage homes, and community kitchens not open to the public
  • All guides are community members — not tour operators, but neighbours who grew up here
  • Handpicked stays: community homestays, zamindari guesthouses, riverside eco-camps
  • 24/7 personal concierge who actually knows your name and your itinerary
  • 100% of community fees go directly to the host families and artisan groups
