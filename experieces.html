Local Life
Local Life & Departure

Become Part of the
Community, Briefly

The most meaningful travel is when the boundary between tourist and local dissolves. You spend your mornings in the market with the fisherman, your afternoons with the weaver.

This isn't observation. It's participation. You leave having contributed something — and received something no souvenir shop can offer.

  • Village immersion with pre-assigned local family hosts
  • Participate in daily rural rituals and harvesting seasons
  • Contribute to community development micro-projects
  • Learn traditional skills: terracotta art, jute weaving, mustard oil pressing
  • Personalised farewell with community ceremony

Day 1

Arrival & City Exploration

Met at Dum Dum airport by your personal guide. Check-in to heritage guest house in South Calcutta. Evening walk through Park Street's colonial lanes and a welcome Bengali dinner.

Heritage WalkBengali CuisineColonial Calcutta
Day 2

Heritage & Culture Deep Dive

Morning at Kumortuli watching masters sculpt the goddess. Afternoon at the Indian Museum and College Street — Asia's largest second-hand book market. Evening boat on the Hooghly.

ArtisansMuseumRiver at Sunset
Day 3

Local Life & Departure

Early morning at Mullick Ghat flower market — Calcutta at its most alive. Cooking class with a local family. Afternoon free for final exploration. Evening transfer with a full heart.

Flower MarketCooking ClassFarewell
Day 1–2

Arrival & Heritage Quarter

Settle in at a colonial mansion guest house. Full-day walk through North Calcutta's lane culture, visiting heritage havelis, marble palaces, and century-old sweetshops.

Marble PalaceNorth CalcuttaSweets
Day 3

Bishnupur Temple Town

Day trip to Bishnupur — a terracotta temple town from the Malla kings. Watch artisans hand-build the famous Bankura horse and browse hand-loom Baluchari sarees.

TerracottaWeavingTemple
Day 4–5

Village Stay & Farewell

Overnight in a rural homestay by a village pond. Fish with the family at dawn. Cook your own meal. Leave with handmade gifts, local songs, and stories that last a lifetime.

HomestayFishingCommunity Meal
Day 1–2

Calcutta Foundations

Deep dive into Calcutta's layered identity — colonial, cultural, and culinary. Visit the writers' buildings, Victoria Memorial, and Rabindranath Tagore's ancestral home.

Victoria MemorialCulture
Day 3–5

Rural Bengal Deep Dive

Three nights in two different villages — one on a river, one near terracotta artisan clusters. Learn pottery, harvest mustard, cook over wood fires, and sleep under a canopy of stars.

PotteryRiver VillageStars
Day 6–7

Sundarbans Edge & Return

Boat through the mangrove delta. Spot wildlife at dawn. Visit a honey-collector community. Return to Calcutta for a farewell dinner, handmade gifts, and warm goodbyes.

SundarbansMangrovesFarewell
